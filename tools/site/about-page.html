Our story

Why SewCraftly exists

A pattern you love…

Every sewist knows the feeling: you find the perfect dress or pair of shorts, print the pattern, lay out the fabric… and then the instructions leave you guessing. Which seam first? Which way does the dart go?

…and instructions that don’t help

Too many patterns come with a few short lines of text, missing sizes, or no fabric amounts at all. That is where good projects end up unfinished in a drawer.

So we started SewCraftly

We wanted a place where every pattern comes with the full picture: a size chart you can trust, fabric amounts per size, notes on fabric and fit, and steps written in plain words.

One pattern at a time

Today we keep adding new free patterns for women, men and kids. Each one is prepared with the same care, and each one is free to download.

“A pattern is only as good as the instructions that come with it.”

The team and what each of us does

Every SewCraftly pattern passes through three pairs of hands before it reaches you.

Pattern maker

Sizes that fit

Prepares each pattern in every size, checks the size charts and finished measurements, and packs the A4, US Letter and A0 files.

Guide writer

Steps in plain words

Writes the sewing steps, the fabric notes and the illustrated guides, so every seam, dart and hem is explained.

Designer

Easy to find, easy to read

Designs the covers, the Pinterest pins and the pages of this site, so you can spot the right pattern at a glance.
